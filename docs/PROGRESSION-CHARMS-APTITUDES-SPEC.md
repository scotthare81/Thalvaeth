# Progression, Charms and Aptitudes specification

## Purpose

Thal'vaeth replaces WoW levels, talents, item levels and rarity ladders with four persistent progression axes:

1. **Knowledge** — what the Remnant has learned.
2. **Gear** — what persistent equipment has been built and improved.
3. **Aptitudes** — learned personal capabilities.
4. **Charms** — limited equipped modifiers that shape a build.

Progression should widen decisions and competence without turning into percentage-stat sludge.

## Aptitudes

Aptitudes are persistent learned capabilities, not a conventional talent tree.

Good aptitude effects:
- unlock a new deliberate action;
- improve an existing survival process qualitatively;
- permit a new interaction/crafting method;
- alter a meaningful tradeoff;
- create a new route through a problem.

Avoid chains of `+2% damage`, `+3% health`, generic crit or item-level scaling.

Aptitudes have stable keys, explicit prerequisites and server-authoritative ownership.

Possible families may include fieldcraft, endurance, apothecary, weapon handling and observation, but exact catalogue is content work.

## Charms

V1 has **two logical Charm slots**.

A Charm is persistent gear/aptitude equipment with:
- stable charm key/instance;
- known effect;
- authored tradeoff where appropriate;
- worn bulk 0;
- server-owned slot state;
- death persistence.

Charms should change how the player approaches a run, not merely rank numerical power.

Examples of design shapes, not locked content:
- quieter deliberate movement at a cost elsewhere;
- better recovery under a specific condition;
- stronger interaction with one crafting/survival behaviour;
- altered risk/reward around wounds or Vigor.

No rarity colours, stars, random affix rolls or charm item-level ladder.

## Acquisition

Aptitudes/Charms can come from discoveries, diagrams, Keepers, district milestones or crafted/restored objects according to authored content. They are not random creature loot by default.

## Swapping

Gear owns two Charm slot occupancies. Effect system owns semantics.

V1 should restrict swapping to safe/home/preparation context unless a specific field-swap mechanic is approved. This prevents encounter-by-encounter charm menu optimization.

## Interaction with Journal

Unknown aptitudes/charms obey discovery secrecy. Journal may record known concepts, but hidden future trees/effects must not leak client-side.

## Balance

Power budget should favor horizontal identity:
- capability;
- efficiency under a condition;
- tradeoff;
- specialization.

Gear form remains more important to combat identity than Charm stacking.

## Acceptance

V1 needs a small curated set sufficient to prove two different viable loadout identities, persistence through death, no hidden leakage, safe swapping and no mandatory universal best choice.
