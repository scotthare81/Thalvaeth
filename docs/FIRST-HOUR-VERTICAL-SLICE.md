# First-hour vertical slice

## Goal

Prove the game, not every feature. The first 60–90 minutes must teach the core loop through play without a forced tutorial death or MMO quest chain.

## Opening

Player begins in Thal'vaeth Monastery/personal context with minimal persistent kit.

Teach spatially:
- movement and interaction;
- personal quarters/home safety;
- Journal access;
- stock/loadout concept;
- Crude Dagger/basic tool identity;
- Rotwood departure.

Keep exposition short. The player should enter Rotwood quickly.

## First run teaching beats

The route/content must make these discoveries possible in a natural order without scripting every exact second:

1. **The world listens.** Walking past/near a threat is meaningfully different from running.
2. **Creatures are observed, not UI-labelled mobs.** First sighting begins Journal knowledge.
3. **Gathering costs attention/time.** First raw material goes to the satchel.
4. **Survival pressure exists.** Hunger/Thirst/Vigor become understandable without immediately dominating.
5. **Combat is commitment.** First fight demonstrates Vigor, reach/recovery and noise.
6. **Noise has consequences.** Another creature may investigate rather than magically aggro.
7. **Discovery matters.** A born-known craft or meaningful experiment produces useful knowledge.
8. **Extraction is a decision.** Player can leave with a modest haul rather than being forced deeper.
9. **Home converts risk into progress.** Extracted material enters stock and enables repair/craft/first improvement.
10. **Second run differs.** Same geography, changed population demonstrates the game's run identity.

## Death

Death can occur naturally. Do not force it. If it occurs:
collapse → visible bags drop → fade → authoritative DEAD finalization → run destroyed → wake in personal bed with persistent worn gear condition unchanged.

The player learns loss through consequence, not a tutorial popup.

## First persistent improvement

Within the first successful run or two, the player should be able to make one understandable persistent improvement: repair, simple gear step, station use, knowledge unlock or equivalent.

It must demonstrate: **what I bring home changes what I can do next time.**

## Tutorial UX

Prefer:
- environmental framing;
- concise contextual prompts;
- Journal updates;
- Keeper dialogue only when useful;
- creature animation/audio cues.

Avoid:
- giant tutorial windows;
- glowing quest arrows;
- checklist objectives;
- forced “kill 3 creatures” tasks;
- exact detection meters.

## Validation sessions

Playtest with:
- player given no verbal explanation;
- fresh player unfamiliar with WoW systems;
- player familiar with extraction games.

Record where players fail to understand movement noise, satchel risk, Vigor, extraction and home progression.

## Success criteria

By the end of the slice a player can explain in their own words:
- why walking matters;
- what death risks;
- what persists;
- why extraction matters;
- how materials become progress;
- why another Rotwood run will not be identical.
